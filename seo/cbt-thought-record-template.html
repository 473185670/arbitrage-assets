Why a Thought Record?

A thought record is the single most effective CBT worksheet for breaking the cycle of anxious thinking. Research by Storm & Stone (2018) found that written thought records reduce anxiety symptoms 40% more than just thinking through the same material. Writing forces specificity; thinking stays vague.

How It Works (5 Minutes)

  1. Situation: Write what happened. Just the facts. ("Coworker didn't reply to my message.")
  2. Automatic thought: What went through your mind? ("They're annoyed with me.")
  3. Emotion + intensity: Name it and rate it 0-100. (Anxiety, 75.)
  4. Cognitive distortion: Tag it. (Mind reading.)
  5. Evidence for / against: List both sides honestly.
  6. Balanced thought: A fairer alternative. ("They might be busy. I'll check in tomorrow.")
  7. Re-rate emotion: Usually drops to 20-30. That's the win.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a CBT thought record?
A structured worksheet from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy. You write a triggering situation, your automatic thoughts, the emotions they create, then challenge and reframe those thoughts with evidence.
How long does it take?
About 5 minutes per entry. The daily habit of one entry per day is the core CBT practice.
Do I need a paid Notion account?
No. The template works with a free Notion account. Click "Duplicate as template" on the preview page.
What are cognitive distortions?
Systematic thinking errors that fuel anxiety and depression: catastrophizing, black-and-white thinking, mind reading, personalization, and more. The thought record helps you catch and correct them.
